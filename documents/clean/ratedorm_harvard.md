## Thayer

It was alright to be honest. As a freshman, I literally spent most of my year here and enjoyed it aside from noisy roommates and plumbing problems (srry if my english is not the best, not my first language)

Lived in a single
3 years ago

Overall, living in Thayer has been a great experience, but more because of the people and not necessarily because of the dorm itself. The hallway-style suites are somewhat cramped and there is little room for privacy. The bathrooms are also shared among a dozen or so students, so they are not especially sanitary or accessible. However, the location is brilliant, with easy access to the dining hall (2 min walk) or to most general lectures in the science center (3 min walk).

Lived in a suite
